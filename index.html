Welcome to the Community Guide

Welcome to my community event guide, where I highlight interesting events happening around Winston-Salem. My goal is to help residents discover activities that bring people together and make the community more enjoyable.

Community events are a great way to meet new people, experience different cultures, support local organizations, and spend time with friends and family.

For this edition, I am highlighting an event that celebrates the many cultures represented in the Winston-Salem community.

Featured Event

International Village Food & Music Festival

The International Village Food & Music Festival gives visitors an opportunity to experience international music, food, crafts, and cultural organizations in one location.

The festival will take place at Winston Square Park in downtown Winston-Salem on Saturday, September 12, 2026.

Admission is free, making this an accessible event for individuals, families, students, and visitors.

  • Live music and cultural performances
  • International food
  • Arts and crafts
  • Community organizations
  • Local vendors

Why Attend?

Events like this provide an opportunity to learn about cultures represented throughout the local community. Visitors can enjoy entertainment while discovering new foods, artwork, and community organizations.

Whether you attend with friends or family, the festival offers a relaxed way to spend an afternoon downtown.

Event Details

Date: Saturday, September 12, 2026

Time: 12:00 p.m. - 5:00 p.m.

Location: Winston Square Park

Admission: Free
